The EXCEL file is the data for plotting regime maps in the scaling study (Figure 6 in the main paper)
Numbers in the first line are the parameters of the water thicknesses (ANG)
Numbers in the first column are the parameters of the vibrational strain

Number in each case represent the corresponding simulation outcome (more details on the definitions of simulation outcome can be found in section 3.5 of the paper):
1.00: cavitation
2.00: no ice nucleation
4.00: ice nucleation
