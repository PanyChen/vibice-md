The EXCEL file is the data for plotting regime maps in Figure 2 in the main paper
Numbers in the first line are the parameters of the vibrational frequency (MHz)
Numbers in the first column are the parameters of the vibrational amplitude (ANG)

Number in each case represent the corresponding simulation outcome (more details on the definitions of simulation outcome can be found in section 3.2 of the paper):
0.00: nucleation with cavitation
1.00: cavitation only
2.00: no nucleation
3.00: partial nucleation
4.00: complete nucleation
